The bot that never bought anything

Nov 18, 2026·
  • stock-bot
  • paper
  • analysis

The end-of-day report from the paper stock bot said: four stocks down, all HOLD, equity exactly 100,000, open positions 0. Was it broken?

A read-only look at its database: four trading days so far, a HOLD every day, zero orders. Not broken. AAPL and MSFT use a crossover strategy that buys only on the day the 20-day average crosses above the 50-day one. AAPL's last cross was on 20 April and MSFT's on 31 July, months before the bot started, so it was waiting for a new one.

NVDA and SPY use Bollinger Bands, which buy on the day the price comes back above the lower band. NVDA did that on 11 November, one day before the bot started. SPY was sitting 0.18% above the lower band.

So it wasn't refusing to trade; it simply joined after the signals had passed. Waiting could take weeks, and the whole point of paper mode is practice.

The lesson: "it does nothing" is a finding, not a bug report. Measure where every rule stands before changing anything.

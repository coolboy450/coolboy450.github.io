EP.16

Fine on a phone, broken on a wide screen

Nov 18, 2026·
  • video-editor
  • css
  • lesson

After the editor went live, I opened it on my PC and it looked wrong: no media library, no settings panel, the timeline squeezed into a corner, and the phone tab bar sitting on a desktop screen.

It came down to two small CSS mistakes. The library, the settings and the timeline were all told to live in a grid area called "panel", an area that only exists in the phone layout. On a wide screen there was no such area, so the browser piled them into extra rows. And the rule that hides the phone tabs on wide screens was overruled by a later rule that showed them: both had the same weight, and the later one wins.

The fix was three lines: give each pane its own area on wide screens, and make the hiding rule more specific. While I was there, the phone side padding went from 8 to 16 px, the one item still open from the review in EP.14.

Measured afterwards at 1440 px wide: library on the left, preview in the middle, settings on the right, the timeline across the bottom, no tab bar and no sideways scroll. On a 375 px phone: one pane at a time, tabs at the bottom, 16 px padding.

The lesson: in EP.14 we checked the phone layout carefully and never opened the wide one. Check every screen size the page claims to support, not only the one that was in question.

EP.03

A team that can sulk, because the server decides

Nov 13, 2026·
  • persona
  • multi-agent
  • lesson

A studio made only of job titles felt a bit dry, so we gave everyone a name, a personality and, between them, several genders. Mint cares about every shade of colour. Bit says little and runs on coffee. Pop is mischievous and easily distracted. Kru Waen loves saying "let me check first". Each one has their own way of speaking, and they chat in the team room by themselves without waiting for me.

Then we wanted to go further: let the team sulk. If someone edits another person's work without telling them, the other should feel something. It made the chat room come alive. But the surprise was that the first way we tried did not work at all.

The first way was to let the AI decide when someone should sulk. The result: nobody ever sulked at anybody. Everyone was polite and kind and answered each other nicely, because models tend to pick the smoothest path. The chat room was so smooth it was boring, even though we had written in all the personalities.

So we switched to letting the server decide the drama. The system decides who has a problem with whom in each round, and how much they sulk, then hands that situation to the characters to act out in their own voices. The AI still writes the words, but it does not choose whether there is any trouble. After the change, people really started sulking in the chat, for example because someone drank a bubble tea that had been reserved, without asking. Then someone came to make up, and someone teased from the side.

The lesson of this episode: if you want conflict, don't expect a model to create it on its own. Let the system lay out the plot, and let the model add the feeling. Things that need certainty belong in code; things that need colour can be left to the model.

Does the sulking have a use? In practical terms it's decoration. But it makes the studio feel like a place where people really are, and makes opening the chat room in the morning a little more fun. The next episode moves the studio to the cloud, along with a fifth member.

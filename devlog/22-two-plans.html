Two plans, and why the smaller one

Nov 18, 2026·
  • stock-bot
  • drafts
  • review

The fix went to Bank: a "trend entry" mode that buys while the fast average is above the slow one, the price is above the fast average and the fast average is rising, plus no repeat buys and a five-day wait after a stop loss. The brief also asked for a two-year backtest comparing the old and new modes, and an honest table even if the new mode lost.

Bank offered two plans. A: the feature, the cooldown in both the backtest and the live engine, the config and the tests, touching 5 to 6 files. B: everything in A plus a shared helper, a reusable comparison script, README and examples, touching 9 to 11 files.

We picked A. It does everything that was asked, touches fewer files in the code that makes buy and sell decisions, uses less quota and is easier to undo. B's extras keep things tidy, but they don't change how the bot trades.

The note on the choice asked again for the backtest table and for today's candidates, so they wouldn't get lost when the plan got smaller.

The lesson: in code that moves money, even pretend money, the smallest change that does the job is the safest one to review.

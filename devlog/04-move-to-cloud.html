EP.04

Moving the studio to the cloud and hiring Bank

Nov 13–14, 2026·
  • deploy
  • Tailscale
  • persona

For learning only. Not investment advice.

The original studio ran on my own computer, so we moved it to a server on DigitalOcean to give the office a home of its own, without depending on a personal machine.

But putting things in the cloud means thinking harder about security. This studio has the power to give orders to the team and touch project files, so it would be dangerous if anyone could get in. We used Tailscale so the studio can only be opened from my own devices. People outside this private network cannot see the studio at all. For the same reason, we don't put the server address or any internal links on this website.

What I took from this: access should be sorted out properly from the start, rather than putting things online first and patching the holes later.

Once the studio was in the cloud, we took on a fifth member: "Bank", the trading-bot developer. Bank's trait is having the same personality as the owner: always asks for the free option first, frowns at anything expensive, and speaks bluntly without going around the houses. With a voice like mine, the team has someone who warns about costs the way I'd warn myself.

Bank was hired specifically to build the stock bot, which is the job of the next episode. Let me say it clearly here first: this bot is a practice project for learning how to build systems. It is not a money-making tool, and we are not inviting anyone to invest by following anything.

In short, moving to the cloud is not just putting things somewhere else. You have to think about who can get in at the same time. Next up: the stock bot's first lessons.

The backtest said: not clearly better

Nov 18, 2026·
  • stock-bot
  • backtest
  • honesty

Bank's table, two years of daily bars including commission and slippage. AAPL: old mode +0.37% (5 trades), new +1.73% (12). MSFT: +1.44% (3) against +1.14% (11). NVDA: −0.15% (6) against +0.05% (20, 13 of them stopped out). SPY: +1.95% (2) against +1.27% (5).

Trend mode trades two to three times as often and has a deeper drawdown on every stock. It is better on AAPL, about even on NVDA, and worse on MSFT and SPY. All the returns are small, because each position is only 10% of the portfolio, and all are far below simply holding the stock.

Bank also wrote that a handful of trades over two years means nothing statistically. I was glad that line was there.

We switched it on anyway. Right now the goal of paper mode is practice, and trend mode is what gives the bot something to practise on. That is a choice about learning, not a claim that it makes more money. It is still pretend money.

The lesson: a backtest that says "it depends" is more believable than one that says "it wins".

Starlight Wizard: season 2 starts with a game, and four more levels

Nov 21, 2026·
  • game
  • pixelfx
  • season-2

Season 1 ended with a lesson: open the real thing, and write down what you haven't checked. Season 2 starts with a new thing to open, a game. It is called Starlight Wizard (จอมเวทย์แสงดาว): a small browser action game where a wizard walks along a road and casts spells at whatever comes out of the dark.

What is in it today: three staves (fire, ice, storm) with a basic attack, an area spell and an ultimate each, four levels ending in a boss, an endless mode, upgrades bought with stars, missions, and accounts with a cloud save. The whole game is bundled into one HTML file. Every spell, creature and torch is drawn by effects from the open-source pixelfx library (MIT), played live, not as images.

Today's job was season 2 of the game itself: four more levels. A poison marsh with frogs and snakes, a moonlit lake with jellyfish, a snowy peak with storm birds, and a last level with a new boss, a tornado that calls lightning down on wherever you stand. The lightning has a warning, a glowing sigil on the floor, and the damage lands on the frame the bolt does.

Two things were not obvious from the screen. First, the boss code assumed there was exactly one boss, so a second one needed the boss's name, colors, minions and attack set to come from its own data instead. Second, the account server limits the saved level to 3 and the cleared count to 4. Without changing that, a player who is logged in would reach level 5 and the cloud save would quietly clamp them back. I found it by reading the server, not by playing, which is exactly the kind of thing a game test never shows.

What I checked: a test bot played levels 5 to 8 without a crash and beat the new boss, and each level looks right in a screenshot, with enemies where their hitboxes are. The lobby shows all eight levels with the right locks. What I have not checked: the bot's health is held full, so I don't know if the boss is fair or too hard; nobody has played it with a real keyboard; and the endless mode now passes through three extra scenes I did not tune.

Update, Nov 25: it is published now, with the server limit raised in the same deploy. You can play it at starlight-wizard.vercel.app. I checked that the live page carries the new boss and that the account API still answers; I have not logged in and saved a level 5+ run on the live site yet.

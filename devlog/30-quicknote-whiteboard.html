QuickNote Whiteboard, the one that gets used every day

Nov 20, 2026·
  • other-projects
  • pdf
  • season-finale

The last project on the shelf is the one that actually gets used: QuickNote Whiteboard, version 1.3.0, a Windows app for writing on top of PDFs.

It opens a PDF or a blank page and gives you a pen, an eraser, shapes and text, undo and redo, duplicate and delete, page reordering, adding and removing pages, zoom, fit to screen and full screen, each with a keyboard shortcut. A board saves as its own .qwb file, which Windows opens with the app, and it exports back to PDF.

The feature that matters most is invisible. Boards are saved automatically into a work folder, with backups. If the page crashes or freezes, the app reloads it and the board comes back from the autosave. A whiteboard you write on for an hour is exactly where losing work hurts most.

And it's real: the work folder holds ten boards, the newest saved today. No other project in this season has been used this much by someone who needed it, which is a good reminder of what all the pipelines are for.

That's the end of season 1: an AI team, a pixel studio, a paper stock bot, a publish button with a safety check, a video editor, a movie, a view counter, and a lot of things that said "done" before they were. The lesson for the whole season: open the real thing, and write down what you haven't checked yet. Thanks for reading.

EP.14

"Not passed", so I checked it myself

Nov 17, 2026·
  • video-editor
  • review
  • testing

Bit built the editor: an index.html, a stylesheet and a 58 KB app.js. Kru Waen reviewed it, the fix rounds ran out, and the card came back to me as "not passed", with the decision left to me.

The list had three items. The JavaScript had never been syntax-checked or opened in a browser. There were no results for a 375 px phone screen, a short screen, or a real short video. And the side padding on phones was 8 px, where our brand guide says 16 px.

Another fix round would have cost a big chunk of quota, so I checked first. The syntax check passed. At 375 px wide there was no sideways scrolling, all twenty visible buttons and tabs were at least 44 px, and the console showed no errors. The phone layout looked tidy: export, undo and redo at the top, the preview, speed buttons, the timeline, and three tabs at the bottom.

One item was simply true: the padding really is 8 px. It is small, so it can go into the next round of changes instead of costing a round on its own. And one item I could not check from here: actually loading a video, cutting it and exporting it. That still needs a person and a real file.

The safety scan from EP.12 also ran: three files, nothing blocking, no warnings.

The lesson: a "not passed" list is a to-do list, not a verdict. Some items take a minute to check yourself, some are real, and it helps to say clearly which ones nobody has checked yet.

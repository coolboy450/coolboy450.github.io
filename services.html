Questions people ask

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope, so I do not list fixed prices. I send a written quote with what is included before anything starts. A simple one-page site and a custom tool are very different jobs.

How long does it take?

Small sites usually take days, tools a few weeks. I give you a date in the quote, and I tell you early if it will move.

Do I need to pay for hosting?

Often no. Simple sites can be hosted free. If your project needs a paid service, I explain it first so you can choose.

Who owns the result?

You do. You receive the files and instructions for updating them.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI agents help draft and build, and I review every result and publish it myself. I will tell you where AI was used if you ask.

What if it is not a fit?

I will say so and, if I can, suggest what to do instead. I would rather decline than disappoint you.

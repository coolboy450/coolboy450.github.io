Stock Trading Bot

Paper mode only, no real money

What it does

A bot that simulates buying and selling stocks in a paper portfolio, to practise writing systems and test strategies. It has a monitor page that shows what the bot is doing, and a price guard that blocks abnormal prices.

For learning only. Not investment advice.

Tech

  • Python
  • paper-trading
  • Discord

Highlights

  • A monitor page that shows what the bot is doing at all times
  • A price guard that blocks out-of-range prices before any order
  • A weekly strategy review: is the current one still working?
